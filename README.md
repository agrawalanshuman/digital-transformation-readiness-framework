# Digital Transformation Readiness Framework

A consulting-style diagnostic tool for assessing an organization's readiness to adopt AI and digital technologies across five key dimensions.

Built as a portfolio project targeting Big 4 AI and Digital Transformation consulting roles.

---

## What This Is

Big 4 firms run readiness assessments like this before launching transformation engagements with clients. This framework replicates that methodology in a structured, weighted scoring model.

It takes inputs across 15 criteria, calculates a weighted maturity score per dimension, visualizes the results as a radar chart, and auto-generates recommendations based on gaps identified.

---

## The Five Dimensions

| Dimension | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Data Maturity | Quality, accessibility, and governance of organizational data |
| Technology Infrastructure | Cloud readiness, ERP modernization, system integration |
| Talent and Skills | AI literacy and digital skills across the workforce |
| Process Standardization | How well-defined and documented core processes are |
| Leadership and Strategy | Executive buy-in and clarity of digital vision |

---

## How to Use It

1. Open the Excel workbook
2. Go to the **Input** tab
3. Score each of the 15 criteria from 1 (Not Ready) to 5 (Fully Optimized) using the scoring guide in column E
4. View auto-calculated results in the **Scoring** tab
5. See the radar chart maturity profile in the **Output** tab
6. Review auto-generated next steps in the **Recommendations** tab

Weights for each dimension can be adjusted in the Scoring tab (column E) to reflect industry-specific priorities. They must sum to 100%.

---

## Maturity Levels

| Score Range | Maturity Level |
|---|---|
| 4.5 and above | Optimized |
| 3.5 to 4.4 | Advanced |
| 2.5 to 3.4 | Developing |
| 1.5 to 2.4 | Emerging |
| Below 1.5 | Not Ready |

---

## Sample Scenario Included

The workbook includes a pre-filled sample scenario based on a mid-size life sciences manufacturer with a recent SAP S/4HANA migration. Key findings from this scenario:

- **Technology Infrastructure** scores highest due to recent ERP modernization
- **Talent and Skills** is the primary gap, limited AI literacy and no formal training program
- **Overall Rating:** Developing (score approximately 2.9)
- **Primary recommendation:** Prioritize AI upskilling before scaling any automation or AI initiatives

This scenario mirrors common patterns seen in life sciences and manufacturing transformation engagements.
